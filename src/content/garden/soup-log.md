## Legend

- Buy again?
  - ✅ - yes
  - 🤔 - maybe
  - ❌ - no
  - ? - not yet opened
- Soup, the brand is abbreviated:
  - CC - Campbell's Chunky
  - CL - Campbell's Country Ladle
  - HZ - Heinz
- Nutrition, from the label, per UK FSA front-of-pack "traffic light" bands:
  - 🟢 - low
  - 🟡 - mid
  - 🔴 - high
- Price, per can, `base (sale)`:
  - `base` - regular shelf price
  - `(sale)` - cheapest seen on sale

## Bands

Using UK's traffic light criteria, per 100 g.[^fsa]
Australia has its own [Health Star Rating System](https://www.healthstarrating.gov.au/)
which kinda works, but I want the individual nutrient ratings.

Aussie labels list sodium, not salt. The UK bands are for salt.

> Salt is defined as the total sodium in the product multiplied by 2.5.
>
> - FoP nutrition label guide, p. 15[^fsa]

| Metric                 | 🟢 low |    🟡 mid | 🔴 high |
| ---------------------- | -----: | --------: | ------: |
| Saturated fat (g/100g) |   ≤1.5 |  >1.5–5.0 |    >5.0 |
| Sugars (g/100g)        |   ≤5.0 | >5.0–22.5 |   >22.5 |
| Sodium (mg/100g)       |   ≤120 |  >120–600 |    >600 |

## Why?

The only canned foods I've tried are corned beef, pork (Maling), and tuna.
Then I tried canned soup during a cold night.
It's life-changing.

When you're already battered at night and the usual choice is to go straight to bed,
a nice warm soup is a massive upgrade to my life.

Since then, I would stock up on these canned soups during sales.
